
Cyber crime incidents such as phishing, online fraud, identity theft, and social media account compromise are increasing rapidly. Most victims are non-technical users who face difficulties in:

* clearly explaining what happened,
* converting their experience into a formal cyber crime report,
* understanding the next steps after an incident,
* knowing how to secure their accounts and digital evidence.

Because of this, many incidents are either poorly reported or not reported at all. CyberDesk addresses this gap by providing an AI-driven assistance system.

---

## 2️⃣ Raw User Input → Structured FIR-Style Report

### 🔹 Step 1: Incident Description (Raw Text Input)

The user enters a **free-text description** of the incident, for example:

> “I received a fake bank message and clicked the link. I entered my card details and money was deducted from my account.”

This input may be unstructured, emotional, or incomplete.

---

### 🔹 Step 2: NLP Processing & Information Extraction

The system uses **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** to:

* identify keywords (bank, link, money deducted),
* detect the type of cyber incident (phishing / financial fraud),
* extract useful details such as:

  * type of incident
  * affected service (bank, social media, email)
  * nature of loss (financial / account access)

---

### 🔹 Step 3: FIR-Style Report Generation

Using **Generative AI**, the extracted information is converted into a **formal, structured cyber incident report** similar to an FIR format.

The generated report includes:

* Type of cyber crime
* Incident description (formal language)
* Mode of attack
* Impact and loss details
* Date and time (if provided)
* Suggested cyber crime category

This report can be **copied, downloaded, or submitted** to cyber crime portals.

---

## 3️⃣ Incident Response & Account Security Guidance

### 🔹 Step 4: Incident Classification

Based on the detected incident type (e.g., phishing, account hacking, online fraud), the system triggers a **response guidance module**.

---

### 🔹 Step 5: Actionable Next Steps

CyberDesk provides **step-by-step instructions** such as:

* Immediately changing passwords
* Blocking bank cards
* Contacting the bank or service provider
* Preserving evidence (messages, links, screenshots)
* Reporting the incident on official cyber crime portals

These steps help victims **reduce damage and prevent further misuse**.

---

## 4️⃣ Cyber Security Chatbot Module

### 🔹 Step 6: Awareness & Education

CyberDesk includes an **interactive chatbot** that:

* answers questions like:

  * “What is phishing?”
  * “How can I protect my account?”
  * “What should I do if my Instagram is hacked?”
* explains cyber threats in simple language
* suggests best practices for online safety

The chatbot improves **user awareness and prevention**.

---

## 5️⃣ System Architecture Summary

```
User Input (Raw Text)
        ↓
NLP Processing & Incident Detection
        ↓
FIR-Style Report Generation
        ↓
Incident Response & Security Guidance
        ↓
Cyber Security Chatbot Interaction
```
